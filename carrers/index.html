Career with us

Our success is the result of the awesome work of our team members, who know what they do and can walk the talk. We are always on the lookout for talented persons who can join our team and empower Triz Technologies to be the best ERP implementation partner across the entire country.

Currently Hiring For the Position of

  • SAP Business One Technical Consultant
  • SAP Business One Addon Developer (Techno-functional)
  • SAP Business One Functional Consultant
Experience: 5years
Job Role: SAP Business One Technical Consultant
Desired Candidate Profile:
  1. The candidate should be a B.Tech graduate.
  2. He or she should have familiarity with any SAP Business One SDK.
  3. The candidate must be proficient in C# and Dot Net.
  4. Knowledge of HANA and SQL is an added advantage.
  5. The person should have a basic idea about the processes in retail and manufacturing companies.
  6. The candidate might need to travel within the city to meet clients.
Experience: 5years
Job Role: SAP Business One Addon Developer
Desired Candidate Profile:
  1. The candidate should have a minimum of B.Tech qualification.
  2. The candidate must have worked with any SAP Business One SDK.
  3. He or she should be proficient in either VB.net or C#
  4. Should be able to work with SAP Business One UI and DI API
  5. Should have a working knowledge of the HANA database paradigm.
  6. He or she should also have a basic knowledge of the business processes in a retail or manufacturing company
Experience: 5years
Job Role: SAP Business One Addon Developer
Desired Candidate Profile:
  1. The candidate should be a good speaker.
  2. He or she should have a thorough knowledge of the internal business process of retail and manufacturing companies.
  3. He or she should be able to liaise with the leaders in organisations, analyse their business needs and map them with the capabilities of SAP Business One.
  4. He or she should have experience in gathering the requirements of the client, liaising with the technical team of any ERP company and delivering the project successfully.
  5. The candidate might be required to travel within the city.

Life at Triz Technologies

ZERO percent toxic atmosphere – that’s the first thing that you will notice here at Triz Technologies. In a world where companies project a cool and happy-go-lucky vibe, our motto is work and go home. It’s that simple. You don’t need to attend late-night parties and do other meaningless non-work things. Do your work well, and you will be appreciated.
Our leaders at Triz Technologies will be guiding you in every step. And since we are in the ERP business, we have consciously made sure that the internal business process in our office happens in a systematic and standardised way. You will feel at home working here.
Lastly, you will be getting enough room for growth – that’s for sure. We have a huge expansion plan in the pipeline. We will not be stopping at just being an SAP Business One vendor. Gradually, we will be working with SAP S/4 HANA ERP, SalesForce CRM and data analytics tools like Tableau. It’s an exciting roadmap ahead.
