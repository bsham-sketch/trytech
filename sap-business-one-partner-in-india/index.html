What is SAP Business One?

SAP Business One (SAP B1) is a world-class ERP solution designed for small and mid-sized businesses. It provides a 360-degree view of your organization by integrating:

  • Finance & Accounting
  • Sales & CRM
  • Purchasing & Supplier Management
  • Inventory & Distribution
  • Manufacturing & Production
  • Business Intelligence & Reporting
  • Human Resources

Unlike standalone software, SAP B1 is a single, integrated solution that eliminates silos, reduces manual effort, and gives you real-time insights for better decision-making.

SAP Business One Pricing in India

One of the most common questions businesses ask us is:
“What is the cost of SAP Business One in India?”

The answer: It depends on your business needs. Pricing is influenced by:

  • Number of users and licenses required
  • Modules selected (Finance, CRM, Inventory, Production, etc.)
  • Customization and industry-specific workflows
  • Deployment model (Cloud or On-premise)
  • Support and AMC requirements

Typical SAP B1 Pricing in India:

  • Starter Pack License: Best for small businesses (1–5 users), starting from ₹3 lakhs onwards.
  • Professional License: Full access to all modules, around ₹1,30000 per user (one-time fee).
  • Limited License: Role-specific licenses (e.g., Finance or CRM), approx. ₹70,000 per user.
  • Implementation Costs: Ranging from ₹2 lakhs to ₹20 lakhs depending on scale and complexity.
  • AMC & Support: Around 17% of license cost annually.
