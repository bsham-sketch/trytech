What is SAP Business One Hybrid?

When SAP Business One is located on the cloud as well as the on-premise infrastructure, it is known as SAP Business One hybrid. It will share infrastructure components such as backup and cyber protection systems. Each company will have a dedicated instance of SAP B1 software running on its dedicated servers.
